FAQ

Questions companies ask before their first hire.

Straight answers on speed, cost, and vetting. Anything else, send us your RFP and ask.

How fast can I hire a Travel Director through Event ConnecTD?

Most companies go from submitted RFP to a curated shortlist within 48 hours, and many confirm a hire in 24 to 48 hours. Because you message Travel Directors directly, there is no agency queue between you and the person you want.

What does it cost to hire through the marketplace?

One finder's fee of 18% of the Travel Director's contracted labor, invoiced only after you hire. There is no subscription, retainer, or posting fee. You pay the Travel Director directly for their work; Event ConnecTD never bills labor.

Example: a Travel Director at $450 per day on a five-day program is $2,250 in labor. The finder's fee is $405.

Are the Travel Directors vetted?

Yes. Every profile documents program history, references, specializations, languages, and availability, and profiles are verified before they are listed. You see the full record before you reach out.

What is a Travel Director?

A Travel Director is the onsite lead for corporate meetings, incentive trips, and conferences. They run transportation, hotel and hospitality desks, VIP handling, and day-of logistics so the program runs the way the planner designed it. Event ConnecTD lists experienced freelance Travel Directors and meeting planners.

Can I hire meeting planners as well as Travel Directors?

Yes. The marketplace includes freelance meeting planners for pre-program planning and full program management, alongside Travel Directors for onsite execution. Search by specialization to find either.
