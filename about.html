About Event ConnecTD

Built to solve the sourcing problem in incentive travel and corporate events — faster, more transparent, and entirely on your terms.

The Problem We Solve

Sourcing the right people shouldn't take 20 hours.

Sourcing Travel Directors and meeting planners for corporate programs relies on word-of-mouth, LinkedIn, and personal favors. The result: 10–20+ hours of sourcing per event, no consistent vetting, and high variability in staff quality. The root cause isn't a lack of talent — it's a lack of access and organization.

Our Mission

The open marketplace the industry was missing.

Build the open marketplace for Travel Directors, meeting planners, and the companies that hire them — faster and more transparent than the current system, so great programs always start with the right team.

What Makes Event ConnecTD Different

Not a staffing agency. A marketplace you control.

We don't assign Travel Directors or gate the directory behind a job post or subscription. Planners search, view full profiles, and contact talent directly — with full autonomy over every hiring decision.

Search, Don't Wait

Browse 200+ vetted profiles instantly. No gatekeeping, no job post required — full access to the network from day one.

Hire Direct

Message talent directly and confirm a hire in 24–48 hours. You own the decision and the relationship — start to finish.

No Subscriptions

No retainer, no monthly fee. You pay one transparent finder's fee only when you actually hire — never for browsing.

Our Platform

Every profile is documented, verified, and ready to vet.

Each profile includes documented program history, verified references, specializations, languages, and current availability — so you can shortlist with confidence instead of taking a gamble on a name someone passed along.

How the Fee Works

One transparent finder's fee. Nothing hidden.

An 18% finder's fee on total TD labor cost — billed separately, only after placement. No subscription, no retainer. Event ConnecTD does not handle TD labor billing; clients pay Travel Directors directly and are invoiced only for the finder's fee.
