FAQ

Questions meeting planners ask before joining.

Free to join, direct client relationships, and matches across meetings, incentives, and corporate events.

Is Event ConnecTD free for meeting planners?

Yes. Your professional profile is free to create and free to keep listed. The finder's fee is paid by the hiring company, so nothing comes out of your rate.

What kind of planning work do companies post?

Single-day corporate meetings, multi-day conferences, incentive travel programs, and executive events. Companies hire freelance planners for pre-program planning, vendor and venue coordination, and full program management.

How is this different from a staffing agency?

An agency sits between you and the client and takes a margin on your labor. On Event ConnecTD, companies message you directly, you agree scope and rate with them, and you keep the client relationship. The client pays you; Event ConnecTD invoices the client a finder's fee.

Can I list as both a meeting planner and a Travel Director?

Yes. Many professionals do both. Add your specializations to one profile and you will be surfaced for planning roles and onsite Travel Director roles that match your experience.
