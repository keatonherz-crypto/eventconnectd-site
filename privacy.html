Privacy Policy

How Event ConnecTD collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Event ConnecTD LLC ("Event ConnecTD," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and marketplace (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account & profile data: name, email, company, role, and—for Travel Directors and meeting planners—program history, references, specializations, languages, and availability.
  • Form submissions: details you share through our RFP and contact forms, such as event dates, destination, program size, and your message.
  • Communications: messages you send through the platform or to our team.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages viewed, links clicked, and general interaction with the Service.
  • Device data: browser type, device, and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used to operate the site and understand usage.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the marketplace and match companies with Travel Directors and meeting planners.
  • To respond to RFPs, contact requests, and support inquiries.
  • To create and manage accounts and profiles.
  • To improve, secure, and analyze the Service.
  • To send service-related and, where permitted, marketing communications (you can opt out at any time).
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. How We Share Information

  • Between marketplace participants: to facilitate matching and hiring, relevant profile and program details are shared between companies and the Travel Directors or planners they engage.
  • Service providers: vendors who help us operate the Service (e.g., hosting, analytics, CRM such as HubSpot, email) under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Service.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, remember preferences, and measure usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect functionality.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods may vary by data type and applicable law.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at keaton.herzberg@eventconnectd.com. We will respond consistent with applicable law.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business use by individuals 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International Users

We operate from the United States. If you access the Service from outside the U.S., you understand your information may be processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, by additional notice.

11. Contact Us
